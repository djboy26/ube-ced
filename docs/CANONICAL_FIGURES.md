# Universal Basic Energy: canonical figures and corrections

**The authoritative reference for every number this programme publishes.** Supersedes any figure appearing in earlier UBE documents. Current to Wave 2. Internal, behind the outreach freeze.

## Corrections to previously circulated figures

This programme has published figures that were wrong. They are stated here rather than quietly replaced.

**Tomato: 4.77 kWh/kg and 18 kWh/kg are both withdrawn.** The first was delivered energy for open-field production; the second was greenhouse heating alone at the farm gate, which is the same error pointing the other way. The traced figure, delivered to a person in Denmark, is about 11 kWh/kg.

**Headline floor: 11,953 kWh per person per year is withdrawn.** It could not be reproduced from the model said to have produced it. Superseded by the interval of 6,500 to 9,400.

**Two document identifiers previously cited were wrong**, resolving to unrelated papers on anaerobic digestion and on grain irrigation in China. Both are retracted.
